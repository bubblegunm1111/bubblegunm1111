## ✨ About Me

I'm a passionate Software Developer who lives at the intersection of **Game Development** and **Mobile Architecture**. I love building experiences that are mathematically robust under the hood (thanks to my love for C++) while being vibrant and engaging on the screen.

Whether I'm writing rendering loops for 2D shooters or architecting backend systems for a social media app, I am constantly learning, iterating, and bringing my ideas to life.

* 🎮 **Game Development:** I specialize in C++ and Raylib, building everything from classic arcade clones to action-packed space shooters and physics simulations.
* 📱 **Mobile & Web Apps:** I architect native Android applications using Kotlin, and build full-stack web platforms using JavaScript, React, and robust backend architectures.
* 🌱 **Currently Exploring:** Advanced game engine architectures, CI/CD pipelines, and modern UI/UX design.
